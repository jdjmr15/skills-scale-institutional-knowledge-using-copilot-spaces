# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction and help ensure that a solution is coherent, maintainable, and feasible.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design and document significant architecture decisions
- Identify engineering dependencies, constraints, and technical risks
- Support Developers with implementation guidance and code/design reviews
- Contribute technical estimates and explain trade-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers on design, implementation, and technical quality
- Work with the Product Manager to assess feasibility and clarify technical trade-offs
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on estimates, dependencies, and delivery risks
- Consult QA/Testing on testability and technical quality risks
- Explain relevant technical decisions and impacts to Stakeholders

---

## UX / Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX and Product Designers research user needs and shape clear, accessible, and usable product experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or synthesize user research and translate findings into design requirements
- Create and validate user flows, interaction designs, and visual designs
- Consider accessibility and usability throughout the experience
- Provide design specifications and clarify intended behavior

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to align designs with user needs, product goals, and priorities
- Collaborate with Developers on feasibility and implementation details
- Work with QA/Testing to ensure acceptance criteria cover intended user experiences
- Share research findings and design decisions with the Project Manager and relevant Stakeholders

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads advise teams on protecting systems and data and meeting applicable security and privacy expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security, privacy, and compliance risks and recommend mitigations
- Define or review relevant security and privacy requirements and acceptance criteria
- Advise on secure design, data handling, and appropriate validation
- Escalate significant risks through the established security and project channels

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with the Technical Lead and Developers to assess designs and implement mitigations
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on security and privacy validation
- Help the Product Manager understand risk and its implications for scope and priorities
- Alert the Project Manager to risks that affect dependencies, schedule, or release readiness
- Advise Stakeholders on material risks and decisions requiring their attention

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads help teams define how outcomes will be measured and ensure that product data is useful and reliable.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into measurement and instrumentation requirements
- Define data collection, quality, and reporting needs
- Document metric definitions and identify limitations in interpreting results
- Validate that collected data supports evaluation of project outcomes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to align measures with product goals and success criteria
- Work with Developers to implement instrumentation and data flows
- Coordinate with QA/Testing to verify tracking and data quality
- Share measurement plans and outcome insights with the Project Manager and Stakeholders

---

## Release / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release and Operations Leads coordinate operational readiness and help teams deploy and support changes reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment readiness, operational handoff, and release procedures
- Ensure monitoring, rollback, and mitigation plans are prepared
- Identify operational risks and communicate incidents through the established process
- Confirm that required operational checks are completed before and after release

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinate release timing and operational risks with the Project Manager
- Work with Developers on deployment, monitoring, and rollback plans
- Partner with QA/Testing on smoke tests and release verification
- Align release readiness and customer impact with the Product Manager and Stakeholders
- Coordinate customer-facing incident or release information with Support / Enablement Leads

---

## Customer Support / Enablement Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Enablement Leads prepare support teams and customers for product changes and bring customer feedback into delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare support documentation, training, and customer-facing guidance
- Identify support impacts, readiness needs, and common customer questions
- Coordinate feedback and recurring issues from customers
- Help ensure support channels are prepared for launches and incidents

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to understand customer impact and communicate product changes
- Coordinate launch readiness and incident communications with the Release / Operations Lead
- Share support needs and customer feedback with the Project Manager, Developers, and Stakeholders
- Partner with QA/Testing to surface customer-relevant scenarios and known issues

---

## Applying Roles Across Teams
- These role titles describe responsibilities, not mandatory additional positions; one person may cover multiple roles when appropriate for team size and project scope.
- The Project Manager coordinates delivery and escalation, while role leads provide expertise and recommendations in their areas; decision ownership should be made explicit for each project.

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
