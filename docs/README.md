# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder brings together OctoAcme's guidance for managing cross-functional projects, from validating an idea through delivery and continuous improvement. The documents are intended to make workflows, ownership, and decisions easy to find and apply consistently.

## Process overview

Projects begin by confirming the business need, measurable outcomes, stakeholders, initial risks, and available resources in a one-pager. Once stakeholders agree on priority, success measures are clear, and team capacity is confirmed, the work moves into planning: the team prioritizes and estimates backlog items, defines acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done, and agrees on milestones, dependencies, and a test approach.

During execution, teams deliver small increments and track work on a shared project board from backlog through review, QA, and completion. Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, resources, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize work, and measure results; developers implement and test; QA validates acceptance; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Regular standups, delivery and PM/Product Manager syncs, demos, and stakeholder updates keep progress visible, while blockers escalate from the team through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor.

Risks and dependencies are identified, assessed, assigned owners and mitigations, and reviewed regularly. Teams use a shared status source and communicate progress, next steps, blockers, and decisions needed; security incidents follow the security incident process. Before release, acceptance criteria, CI, and security scans must pass, with release notes, smoke tests, and rollback or mitigation plans prepared. Teams verify deployments after release, and retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents turn lessons into owned, measurable improvement actions.
